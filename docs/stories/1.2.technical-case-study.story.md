## Story

**As a** data-engineering recruiter, **I want** to explore a realistic reproducible dataset, dashboard, and full pipeline trace, **so that** I can understand the project's engineering and business value without reading the repository first.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Seed `42` deterministically creates exactly 101,786 source rows spanning 2024-07-01 through 2026-06-30: 30 suppliers, 150 products, 6 warehouses, 21,600 inventory snapshots, 70,000 sales, 8,000 orders, and 2,000 price-history rows.
2. Synthetic data models seasonality, promotions, demand classes, price changes, delivery delays, inventory risk, and controlled business anomalies while preserving structural integrity.
3. `python -m scripts.orchestration.demo --scale portfolio --seed 42 --output site` runs generation, MySQL load, extract, transform, validate, processed load, aggregation, and publication; `smoke` provides a smaller gate.
4. The legacy `scripts.orquestration` import remains temporarily compatible.
5. The Pages root is an interactive dashboard with period, warehouse, category, and supplier filters; KPI and chart results agree with Python aggregates.
6. `/report/` preserves the detailed quality report; downloadable data includes schema, manifest, seed, and SHA-256 checksum.
7. The case explains ER model, lineage, formulas, stored procedures, stage timings, source/processed counts, real code, decisions, limitations, and local/Codespaces execution.
8. Determinism, counts, FKs, rules, KPIs, filter parity, Docker, desktop/mobile, keyboard, no-network, secret, and PII gates pass.
